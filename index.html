About

I grew up in the Detroit orbit, where the car in the driveway wasn't just transportation. It was the whole story of a family. Status, livelihood, identity. The region ran on it. So did everyone I knew.

After studying communications and advertising in Boston, I took what felt like a slightly ironic first job: writing Buick retail ads at McCann in Detroit. It clarified everything quickly. "I needed a different city, a different industry, a different problem to solve." McCann sent me to San Francisco. I planned to stay two years.

What followed was a decade building growth and brand organizations inside companies trying to change how cities move. Zipcar, GIG Car Share, then IDEO, then Lyft. A career spent persuading people to leave their cars behind, shaped entirely by growing up where those cars were everything.

Now I'm CMO at Siegel+Gale, Omnicom's global brand consultancy, creating and conveying value by embracing constant change.

Philosophy

Marketing is most powerful when brand clarity, the demand engine, creative, and data are all pulling in the same direction. Getting those things aligned is the actual job.

On brand

Brand is the highest-leverage demand gen tool available to most organizations. The leaders who treat brand as a growth decision tend to be the ones winning the next cycle.

On teams

The most important hires I've made were people better than me in specific areas. Recognizing that gap and filling it deliberately. That's most of leadership.
